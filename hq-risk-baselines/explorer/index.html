HQ-Risk Results Explorer

Clean baselines and attack sweeps for the three HQ-Risk scenarios: S1 classical ML, S2 federated learning, S3 QML (the QuantumNet circuit trained with Adam, evaluated on Qiskit Aer at 256 shots), across Breast Cancer, Digits and Wine. Attacks cover the core set (poisoning, evasion, Byzantine clients), ARF (backdoor, steal, infer) and QTF (circuit, shots, noise, TSV transpiler). Every point is 5 seeds.
